## Compliance & Legal Assurance
Both primary font families are released under the **SIL Open Font License (OFL) 1.1**.
Under OFL Section 5:
- Fonts can be bundled, redistributed, and sold with any software.
- The requirement that derivative works remain under OFL applies only to fonts themselves, **not** to the software containing or using them.
- Commercial SaaS deployment, multi-tenant clinic usage, and proprietary CRM distribution are fully permitted at **₹0 licensing cost**.
